Fallow Garden

An anthology of small, restorative utilities.

In agriculture, leaving land fallow is not neglect—it is a deliberate pause that allows depleted soil to naturally replenish itself. Our minds, bodies, habitats, and plans require the same unhurried care.

Each utility is published as a focused, self-contained micro-tool designed around one specific problem. No algorithmic tracking, zero data harvesting, and no ads—open and run them directly in your web browser, or install them to your device as lightweight web apps.
