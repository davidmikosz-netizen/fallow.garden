Practical steps for the fallow time of your post-career pivot

Stepping away from a long-held career is often treated purely as a financial milestone. But as transition expert William Bridges notes, a true transition is actually a three-part journey: it requires an ending, a new beginning, and a crucial "fallow time" in between.

This fallow period is where the real growth happens. While many face what psychologist James Bugental called "the nevers"—the realization that you will never be the big boss, famous, or achieve some other lingering ambition—this transition is actually an opportunity to go far beyond what you imagined were your previous goals.

To make this shift, you need to unlearn the habits you thought made you successful. Promotions, productivity, and how busy you are no longer prove your worth (if they ever truly did).

Next Chapter customizes your dashboard based on:

  • ✦ Your Timeline: Whether you are three years out, six months away, or already transitioned.
  • ✦ Your Depth: Choose from practical daily checklists, middle-path exploration, or deep reflection.
  • ✦ Your Focus: Tackle daily routines, dormant hobbies, social networks, or personal logistics.

This app won't provide the Answers, but hopefully it will spark the right questions so you can define a meaningful life on your own terms.
