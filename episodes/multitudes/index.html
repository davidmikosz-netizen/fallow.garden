Natural History of your Gut Biome

The Multitudes Within

Your body contains more beneficial bacteria than human cells. You are the caretaker of an entire ecosystem—and your gut is your body's most underrated, highly-sensitive organ. This app explores the mechanics of your digestive tract, provides a simulator to see how your daily choices affect your internal environment, and offers a field guide to your keystone microbial players.

🌿 About fallow.garden & Feedback

fallow.garden is an independent anthology of single-purpose micro-utilities created to counter cognitive fatigue, algorithmic noise, and relentless optimization culture. Each tool runs entirely on your device with zero tracking or account friction.
