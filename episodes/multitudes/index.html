Natural History of your Gut Biome

The Multitudes Within

“Do I contradict myself? Very well then I contradict myself, (I am large, I contain multitudes.)”

This app contains an explanation of what is happening inside your digestive tract, followed by an interactive simulator to see how your daily choices affect your internal ecosystem. Lastly, we provide a field guide introducing all the main microbial players and metabolic currencies.

Titled after Walt Whitman’s Song of Myself and inspired by Ed Yong’s seminal work I Contain Multitudes: The Microbes Within Us and a Grander View of Life.

An introduction to the science of your gut biome can sometimes feel a bit overwhelming. You can choose between three different depth levels: Level 1 (Meadow) offers an easy-to-understand intuitive introduction; Level 2 (Physiology) dives into systems physiology and cellular mechanics; and Level 3 (Deep Taxa) lets you truly geek out on the underlying scientific taxa and biochemistry.
