When to Deploy a Protocol

Attention fatigue rarely announces itself loudly—it shows up as micro-frictions, compulsive tab opening, and phantom urges. Step away for a protocol the moment you observe these behavioral signals:

Symptom 01 / Optical

The Glaze & Loop

You catch yourself reading the exact same paragraph or email three times without retaining a single concept.

Symptom 02 / Habitual

The Phantom Check

Your hand reaches for your phone or opens a social/news tab without any conscious decision or intent.

Symptom 03 / Latency

The Micro-Tab Spree

You open two or three secondary tabs simply because a primary page takes more than two seconds to load.

Symptom 04 / Cognitive

Friction Avoidance

A complex task becomes momentarily difficult or ambiguous, and you immediately flee into shallow administrative inbox checks.

Symptom 05 / Bandwidth

Attention Thrashing

Bouncing between chat, email, spreadsheets, and drafts while completing zero meaningful, coherent units of work.

Symptom 06 / Circadian

The Midday Energy Dip

Your natural post-lunch circadian trough hits, leaving your directed attention depleted and reactive.

Recovery Protocols

Active tools designed to disengage Directed Attention and induce Soft Fascination without digital friction.

3 Minutes

Optical Soft Reset

Relax ciliary eye muscles and engage panoramic vision with the expanding rhythm.

03:00
Gaze past your display. Follow the gentle pulse and let your peripheral vision soften and expand.
5 Minutes

Zero-Input Rest

Activate the Default Mode Network (DMN). No reading, scrolling, or incoming stimuli.

05:00
Step back or close your eyes. Allow fleeting thoughts to drift through like clouds without catching them.
15 Minutes

Nature Walk & ART

Attention Restoration via effortless observation of natural movement and depth.

15:00
"Observe the motion of tree branches or clouds against the sky."

The Open Loop Catcher

Use this each time you open Fallow Garden. Never let an impulse break your deep focus. Offload stray thoughts to clear working memory, then review them after your fallow rest. This will reset every time you use the app.
