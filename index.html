fallow.garden

Small, quiet web tools that don't watch you back.

Most software is built like a slot machine—designed to harvest your clicks, ping your nervous system, and sell your attention. This is the opposite: a small collection of single-purpose tools for resting your brain, tending your gut, stewarding native plants, and planning the next chapter of your life. Everything runs locally in your browser. Free, with no accounts, no tracking.
