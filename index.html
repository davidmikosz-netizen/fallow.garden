Fallow Garden

In agriculture, letting a field lie fallow is a deliberate act of renewal. In digital life, it is about stepping back from endless feeds and monolithic platforms.

This is an anthology of small, single-purpose interactive tools and experiments—published as standalone episodes. Each tool is built to solve one specific problem cleanly, run directly in your browser, and step aside.
