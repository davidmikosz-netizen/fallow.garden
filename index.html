Fallow Garden

An anthology of small, restorative utilities.

In agriculture, leaving land fallow is not neglect—it is a deliberate pause that allows depleted soil to naturally replenish itself. Our minds and bodies need the same unhurried rest. This space offers a collection of tools for different dimensions of recovery and regeneration.

Each tool is published as a self-contained episode designed around one specific need. The episodes require no setup and contain no distractions: open and run them directly in your web browser, or install them to your device as lightweight, focused apps.
